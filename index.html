QuantCouncil Publisher

A private desktop utility for delivering research files to the operator's Google Drive.

QuantCouncil Publisher runs on Timothy Kern's Windows computer. It checks locally collected research, uploads a report to a selected private Google Drive folder, and updates a selected index file so the operator's Council workflow can find the report.

Google Drive access

The utility requests Google's drive.file permission. Access covers files the operator selects for this app and files the app creates. It checks the destination's ownership and sharing settings and reads uploaded content back to confirm delivery.

Reports remain in the operator's authorized Drive account. This website does not connect to that account, display its files, or offer a public sign-in service.

Scope of the utility

The publisher transfers research files. Google authorization for this utility does not grant broker access or permission to place trades. Any Council interpretation or trading controls belong to separately configured workflows.

See the privacy policy for data use and retention, and the terms for the conditions of this private utility's use.
