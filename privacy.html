Privacy policy

QuantCouncil Publisher · Updated September 27, 2026

Operator and purpose

Timothy Kern operates QuantCouncil Publisher for personal research-file delivery. Questions or requests about this utility can be sent to timothykern62@gmail.com.

Google data accessed and used

With the operator's consent, the utility uses Google Drive file identifiers, names, parent-folder identifiers, ownership and sharing information, version information, and the contents of selected or app-created research and index files. It uses that information to check destinations, deliver research files, verify exact content, and recover an interrupted delivery.

Its Google permission is limited to drive.file. This permission does not provide general access to every file in the account. The utility uses fixed private destinations configured by the operator.

Storage and retention

Research files and delivery records are kept on the operator's Windows computer and in the selected Google Drive location until the operator removes them. The saved publisher refresh credential is encrypted on the Windows computer and is used to renew access without asking for consent on every delivery. Authorization tokens are not included in generated research reports.

Sharing and service providers

The utility sends authorized file operations to Google. It does not make reports public, sell Google user data, or use that data for advertising. The operator can separately authorize a ChatGPT Business Council workflow to read the private reports; that access is governed by the operator's separate Google connection and ChatGPT workspace settings. This static website does not receive Drive files or OAuth credentials.

Google user data is used for the delivery functionality described here. It is not used by this utility to train a general-purpose AI model.

Website hosting

If these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website requests under its own privacy statement. These pages include no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or Google account sign-in.

Control and removal

The operator can stop the utility, remove its Google account authorization, and delete local credentials, local records, and Drive reports. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete existing local or Drive files. Contact the operator with questions about removal or retention.
